Live draft-day board: search, filter, sort, mark picks, and track your own team’s category needs in real time. Works fully offline.

02

Punt planner

Ranks which categories a roster is already losing most, and re-ranks the full player pool with one category’s weight zeroed.

Open tool
03

Trade finder

Searches every one-for-one swap in the league for trades that improve both sides’ expected categories won per week.

Open tool
04

Buy low, sell high

Diffs real in-season per-game performance against each player’s preseason projection, weighted the same way the model values categories.

Open tool
05

Trade analyzer

Full per-category before and after breakdown for one proposed trade, expected categories won per week, both sides, against the league field.

Open tool
06

Schedule strength

Real games per team for any fantasy week: who to stream, who to sit, straight from the live NBA schedule.

Open tool
07

Projected standings

Projects each team’s final win-loss-tie record and league rank from the real weekly schedule, blending results already played with simulated results for the weeks ahead.

Open tool
08

Championship odds

Monte Carlo odds of finishing first: each matchup’s win probability is simulated once, then the whole season is resampled thousands of times to see how often each team comes out on top.
